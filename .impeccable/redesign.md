# SGB Pulse — floating island

Mode: native Windows Operate. Public distribution, local per-user account discovery, read-only provider integrations, compact daily monitoring. The user's latest direction supersedes the earlier taskbar and broad row designs.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A small native companion with three distinct placements; show identity and the relevant quota at a glance, then reveal one account's detail.
OWN-WORLD: Charcoal #13181B, sage #A4DCBD, colored quota arcs, native Segoe, a rounded island or a circular orbit around SGB. Restrained motion and small, readable controls.
STORY: The installation finds its own supported local accounts, each product retains its own quota, the user positions the island, then opens one account for actual limits and a conditional pace recommendation.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Three selectable presentations. Dynamic Island touches the physical top edge at y0, with square upper corners and14-DIP lower corners; icons and percentages at rest expand on native pointer entry into full names, separate limits and resets. Floating mode is a214-DIP circle in every style, with perimeter quota sectors, inner provider marks/percentages and a central pulse grip. Taskbar defaults to thin quota rings with provider marks in their centers, readable names and a second line of values. When space is tight, fewer named providers plus +N preserve identity. The410-DIP panel shows a plan badge such as Pro or Max20x on every collapsed provider row, plus expandable limits/advice.
FORM: Code-led native WPF local extension with precisely pinned edge geometry and the user's original taskbar preference; no concept tournament or external card. Signature interaction: the top-attached shell really grows on hover and returns on departure without losing its anchor. Native shown-window tests, not only offscreen state changes, verify dimensions, pointer events, data refresh and animation. Windows reduced motion is respected; a test-only environment delegate exercises animation without changing OS settings. Dragging saves a free position and refreshes its corners. Floating sectors carry separate actual quotas.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Truth and boundaries
Cursor's own billing-period service and Grok Bot's weekly service stay separate even for the same email. Public defaults discover this Windows user's supported local accounts; no personal mail receipt or credential ships. Unknown or old readings use an em dash. Forecasts use actual reported period dates and clearly state their uncertainty; absent period dates do not produce an invented end day.

## Required native evidence
Compact collapsed and selected-account panel; 100/150 percent renders; all three island styles; appearance, connections and system settings; Save success/failure and reload; automatic discovery preserving opt-outs; hover start/stop and reduced motion; live product readings. Native Windows skips the HTML/CSS detector. Memory and CPU are measured separately from visual review. Public package excludes settings, cached accounts and real-account captures.
